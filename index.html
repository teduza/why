About the Project

Private AI that lives on the device

M.A.R.S. Companion (Mobile Autonomous Reasoning System) is a fully offline voice AI companion with long-term semantic memory. Speech recognition, reasoning, and speech synthesis all run locally — nothing leaves the device.

No accounts, no cloud, no data collection. A personal AI that belongs to its owner, not a corporation.

Prototype

A working prototype exists today

The system runs the full offline pipeline — speech recognition, semantic memory retrieval, contextual reasoning, and speech synthesis — entirely locally, on a Raspberry Pi 4.

Tested by the founder and other users.
